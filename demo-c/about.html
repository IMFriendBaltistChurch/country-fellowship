Doctrine

What We Believe

We hold to the historic Christian faith as expressed in Scripture, identifying with the 1963 Baptist Faith and Message as our doctrinal standard.

The Bible

"The Scriptures of the Old and New Testament as the inerrant and inspired word of God and as the final authority in faith and life."

The One True God

"One God who eternally exists in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit."

The Lord Jesus Christ

"Jesus Christ is the only begotten Son of God, born of the virgin Mary, lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, was resurrected from the dead, and is the only mediator between God and man."

Salvation

"All who receive the Lord Jesus Christ by faith are born again of the Holy Spirit and thereby become children of God."

Ordinances

We practice believer's baptism by immersion and observe the Lord's Supper as ordinances for those who profess faith in Jesus.

The Church

A voluntary community of baptized believers joined together for worship, instruction, and service — emphasizing stewardship and discipleship.

Marriage

"Marriage is a union between one man and one woman ordained by God as delineated in Scripture."

Baptist Faith & Message

We identify with the 1963 Baptist Faith and Message as a guide to our doctrinal commitments and statement of faith.

Note: These statements reflect the core doctrinal commitments of Country Fellowship Church. For a full copy of the Baptist Faith & Message, contact us directly.

Our Team

Leadership

👤

Charles Higgs

Founding Pastor

Pastor Charles Higgs brings decades of ministerial experience across multiple churches to Country Fellowship. His heart is to help people — especially those in the second half of life — find meaning, purpose, and deep rootedness in God's Word.

He previously served as Director of Western Heritage Ministry for Texas Baptists, overseeing approximately 150 church plants across the region. That experience in planting and sustaining healthy churches deeply shapes the culture here at Country Fellowship.

Country Fellowship Church was founded on his driving conviction: to help every believer "Finish Well for Jesus."
