Welcome to Country Fellowship

You Belong Here

A Place to Meet Jesus & Find Community

Country Fellowship Church is a place where people can meet Jesus, engage in life-giving community, and everyone is welcome. No matter where you're starting from — new to faith, returning, or just exploring — you are welcome here.

"We are a community rooted in the Word, committed to one another, and passionate about finishing well for Jesus."

Our Sunday services run about 60 minutes and include a blend of contemporary and traditional music, a message grounded in Scripture, and time for prayer. Come as you are.
