Why We Give

God is generous — and so He calls us to be as well. Generosity is an act of trust and worship. When you give, you partner with God's work in our community, our city, and the world.

Your giving sustains Sunday gatherings, outreach, missions partnerships, and the ongoing work of discipleship right here in Bryan, TX.
