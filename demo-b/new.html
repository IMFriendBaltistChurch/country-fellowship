Welcome to Country Fellowship

You Belong Here

A Place to Meet Jesus & Find Community

Country Fellowship Church is a place where people can meet Jesus, engage in life-giving community, and everyone is welcome. Whether you're new to faith, returning after time away, or just curious — we're so glad you're here.

"We are a community rooted in the Word, committed to one another, and passionate about finishing well for Jesus."

Our Sunday services last about 60 minutes and include a blend of contemporary and traditional music, a Scripture-centered message, and time for prayer. Come as you are — no special attire required.
